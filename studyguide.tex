\noindent\textbf{How to use this guide:} Each section is organized top-down. Read the grey and green boxes first --- that's your conversational ammunition. Orange boxes are depth for if they push. Equations are in the appendix only. You're preparing for a \textit{technical conversation between two systems thinkers}, not a midterm.

\noindent\textbf{Structure:} \textbf{Sections 1--6} (including 5.5) are your technical foundation (RL pipeline, reward hacking, scaling, Constitutional AI, the verification bottleneck pattern). \textbf{Sections 7--10} are interview tactics (hard questions, research proposals, depth questions, project strategy). \textbf{Sections 11--12} are deep dives if the conversation goes technical on scaling laws or hardware. \textbf{Sections 13--14} are quick reference. Read in order first, then revisit by need.
